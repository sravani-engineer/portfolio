Hand Gesture Recognition — Real-World Failure Analysis

Built a real-time ML system and analyzed performance under distribution shift.

  • 📊 Evaluated on 12 sessions, 5 users, ~22K frames
  • 🔬 Used session-based validation to prevent data leakage
  • 📉 Identified ~12% performance drop in real-world conditions
  • ⚠️ Discovered major failure: open vs four confusion

Impact:

  • Exposed hidden overfitting in standard evaluation methods
  • Demonstrated real-world performance drop under domain shift
  • Identified feature-level limitations in gesture classification

Key Insight: Model errors were driven more by gesture similarity than environmental variation.

Most ML models fail silently in production — I build systems that expose those failures.
